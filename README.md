## 🥖About

I am a **Computer Science undergraduate at Ionian University** and an **AI/ML research assistant** working across machine learning, deep learning, explainable AI, natural language processing, and retrieval-augmented generation.

At the **Humanistic and Social Informatics Lab (HILab), Ionian University**, I work on transformer-based NLP, interpretable machine learning, and scientific AI. My research includes [a legal-domain BERT model for named-entity recognition and anonymization](https://nikolaosroufas.me/#pub-legner), [a layer-wise study of transformer explainability](https://nikolaosroufas.me/#pub-bigdata), [an efficient-attention approach to protein-structure prediction](https://nikolaosroufas.me/#pub-protein), and [an investigation of expressivity, trainability, and gradient scaling in variational quantum classifiers](https://nikolaosroufas.me/#pub-quantum).

During my **Erasmus+ exchange at Sapienza University of Rome**, I conduct research at the **PSSM Lab, DIAG**, under **Prof. Massimo Mecella**, where I lead the development of [LegalSENN, an intrinsically interpretable transformer architecture for legal document classification](https://nikolaosroufas.me/#pub-legalsenn).

I have also worked in industry as an **AI researcher**, evaluating large language and multimodal models and building reproducible **PyTorch-based evaluation tooling**.

My research has resulted in **four peer-reviewed publications** and **two manuscripts currently under review**. I am broadly interested in building AI systems that are accurate, transparent, and reliable enough for real-world deployment.
